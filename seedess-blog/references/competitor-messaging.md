# Competitor messaging

How each vendor talks to the same reader, and what they leave open. Use this to position without
attacking; never misquote a competitor's price or claim.

| Vendor | Lane | Their message | What they leave open | How we answer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Bunny** | Cheap per-GB CDN | $0.01/GB NA/EU, 119 PoPs, no request fees — price is the whole argument | Rate is one meter; bytes, fill and redundancy untouched | "Rate is lever one; we are lever four" |
| **Cloudflare Stream** | Unmetered + managed video | $1 per 1,000 minutes delivered, no egress fees | Delivery minutes still scale with viewers | "A lower meter is still a meter" |
| **CloudFront / AWS** | Default | Bundle, free tier, enterprise comfort | Egress is the line finance notices | "The default is the bill" |
| **Fastly** | Performance CDN | Speed, instant purge, edge compute | Highest $/GB; savings are not the pitch | "We make the bytes cheaper, not the CDN" |
| **Mux** | Video infrastructure | DX, player, QoE data, spending alerts | Cost is monitored, not removed | "Alerts tell you it is happening; offload changes it" |
| **Lumen / Streamroot** | OTT peer-assisted | 55–85% delivery-cost reduction, enterprise sales | Quote-only, opaque, slow to try | "Same idea, measurable, self-serve" |
| **Hive / Kollective / Microsoft eCDN** | Enterprise internal video | Peak bandwidth control for Teams/events | Different buyer and problem | "Not our lane; do not compare" |
| **Novage P2P Media Loader** | Open source | No server software, public trackers, cut CDN expenses | No measurement, support or bill reconciliation | "Free mechanism, no accountability" |
| **CDNBye** | Open source | "Let your viewers become your unlimitedly scalable CDN" | Commodity, no support | "The price floor, not the product" |

## The gap that is our position

Nobody sells **measured savings on the buyer's existing player and CDN, with a number finance can
audit.**

- Open source gives the mechanism without accountability.
- Enterprise vendors give accountability without self-serve access.
- CDNs sell the meter they profit from.

Position: *the only lever measured on your own bill, stacked on whatever you already run.*

## Claims about competitors

Allowed:
- "Lumen/Streamroot claim 55–85% delivery-cost reduction." (vendor claim; source in market research)
- "Novage claims up to 80% VOD bandwidth savings." (vendor claim)
- "CDNBye is free and open source." (fact)
- Prices from the provider's own page, dated.

Not allowed:
- "Cheaper than X" without the region, tier and date.
- "They don't work" — they do; the difference is packaging and measurement.
- Any competitor's private pricing or speculation about their contracts.

## Where we deliberately do not compete

Enterprise internal video (Hive, Kollective, Microsoft), DRM-strict premium catalogues, betting-grade
sub-second latency, and mobile native players. Say so in the posts; the honesty is the differentiation.
